# Kailash

### Founded by Swami Shivananda Giri Maharaj

###### The Guru founded a social service institution in the name of his disciple, which is an unique and unparalleled event that remains ever glorious.

##### On the 30th April, 1978, after the conclusion of the first Nikhil Banga Bhakti Geeti Sammelan, Maharaj invited all the artists and devotees on that day at the temple of Maa Bhavatarini, Dakshineswar. The purpose was to partake the sacred Prasad together.

## Swami Shivananda Giri Maharaj(1936 - 1987)

### In the year 1936, 23rd April, Swami Shivananda Giri Maharaj was born on the morning of Thursday. This divine manifestation occurred at Ananda Ashram, located at 21/2 Beadon Street. His birth took place on the sacred lunar day of Akshaya Tritiya.
