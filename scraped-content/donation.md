## Donate & Save Tax

Please send your information regarding donation to us through mail at kailashssgm@gmail.com for getting Money Receipt and 80G Exemption Certificate.

Certificate U/S 10BE of IT ACT will be issued for donations of Rs.1000/- and above only against their PAN/AADHAR 10BE certificate under section 80G will be sent to you after getting from Income Tax Department, Govt. of India.

**Please note:** PAN details are mandatory for donations amounting to ₹10,000/- or more in a financial year.

### Donation Form ("Donor Details")

1. First Name * (text, name="first-name")
2. Last Name * (text, name="last-name")
3. Full Address * (text, name="address")
4. Country * (select, name="country") — options: India, Other
5. Other Country (select, name="other_country", hidden unless "Other" selected) — full country dropdown list
6. PIN Code * (text, name="pincode", max/min length 6)
7. Mobile * (tel, name="mobile", max/min length 10)
8. Email * (email, name="email")
9. Donor PAN (If required 80G certificate) — radio (name="pan_required"): No / Yes
10. PAN Number (text, name="pan_number", hidden field shown when "Yes" selected above) — placeholder "Enter PAN Number"
11. Purpose of Donation * (select, name="purpose") — options: Select Purpose of Donation (placeholder), Shivananda Snehanir, Vivekananda Snehanir (Foster Care Unit), General Donation, Aid to weaker and ailing, Educational Aid, Festival and Ceremonies

13. Static note text: "You will be acknowledged within 3 working days through the above email."
