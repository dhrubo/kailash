## Our Activities

### Kailash at Beadon Street

#### Social Activities

### Homeopathy Clinic Providing accessible homeopathic healthcare and basic medical support to those in need.

### Eye Clinic Offering eye care and vision support to help improve the health and wellbeing of the community

### Shivananda Snehanir

### Free Educational Support to students from various local schools by distributing books, school uniforms, school bags and other educational items

### Music & Cultural Activities Encouraging children to express themselves through music, singing and creative participation.

### Free Homeopathy Clinic for health check up with medicines

#### Annual Festivals & Ceremonies

### Sree Guru Purnima A sacred celebration honouring the Guru through prayer, devotion and spiritual observance.

### Vivekananda Snehanir

### Growing Through Play, Friendship and Joy — the boys of the Foster Care Unit enjoying a lively game of football, nurturing teamwork, confidence, discipline, and the simple joys of childhood through play and sportsmanship

### Nurturing Talent,Inspiring Young Hearts — the boys of the Foster Care Unit joyfully immersed in music, discovering the beauty of learning, creativity and self-expression in an atmosphere of care, encouragement, and togetherness

### Excursion to Kolkata Educational and recreational outings that give children opportunities to explore, learn and experience new places.

### Distribution of blankets, clothings, foods to underprivileged and needy people of the adjoining places.

### Health Check-Up Camp Providing basic health check-ups to support the wellbeing of children and families.

### A joyful day of togetherness for the boys of Vivekananda Snehanir Foster Care Unit, exploring Kolkata.

### A Proud Milestone for Vivekananda Snehanir — Where Discipline Blossoms into Achievement.

### Holi Celebrating the festival of colours with joy, togetherness and shared moments of happiness.

### Birthday Celebration Making every child’s birthday special through celebration, companionship, fun and food.

### Yoga and Sports Day Encouraging physical fitness, discipline and wellbeing through yoga, sports and recreational activities.

### Saraswati Puja Celebrating the goddess of knowledge and learning through prayer, devotion and community participation.
